The Art of Sourdough

How We Bake Each Morning

We believe in structural integrity, wild-fermentation, and high hydration. Each loaf takes nearly two full days to complete, requiring patience and skilled hands.

1

Wild Culture Feeding

Every morning begins with refreshing our decade-old sourdough culture. We feed it organic grain flours and warm water to peak fermentation before mixing.

2

The Autolyse & Hand Stretch

We mix stoneground flour and water alone to begin gluten development. Over the next several hours, we fold and stretch the dough gently by hand.

3

Cold Retardation & Scoring

Once shaped, loaves spend twenty-four hours resting in wicker bannetons inside our cold room. This develops the signature tangy sourdough depth of flavor.

4

Stone-Hearth Scorching

We score each loaf with a razor blade and bake directly on the heavy stone hearths with dense steam. This gives our loaves a wild expansion and a dark, crackling shell.
