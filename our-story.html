Our Wild-Fermented Journey

Born from heat, flour, and fifty years of wild yeast.

At Rise & Crumb Sourdough, we do not believe in shortcut baking. Our entire bakery revolves around a single, vibrant sourdough starter born in 1976, nurtured daily with organic flour and cool mountain well water.

36-Hour Slow Rise

Time develops the deep complex sugars, making every crumb remarkably tender and easy to digest.

Radiant Stone Ovens

Heavy fire clay decks deliver intense bottom heat, creating that signature blistered, mahogany crust.
