30 Hour Rise Process

We believe beautiful bread requires only three elements: flour, water, and salt.

No commercial yeast, zero additives, and absolutely no shortcut preservatives. Every golden loaf that leaves our stone deck oven is naturally leavened by our active heirloom sourdough starter, nurtured daily in our warm bakery kitchen.
